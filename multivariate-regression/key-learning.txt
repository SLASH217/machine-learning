if you fit with columns like this:
  reg.fit(df[['area', 'bedrooms','age']], df.price)
then you also need to predict with columns in the same order:
  reg.predict(pd.DataFrame([[3000,3,40]],
            columns=['area','bedrooms','age']))
otherwise a warning is displayed

Here is multivariate regression there are many independent variables used to predict the dependent variable.

In this case the dependent we are trying to predict using area, bedrooms and age is salary.
